Case Study: Building the CPD App

How the CPD App bridges the gap between CPD logs, competency frameworks, evidence and development planning.

Introduction

The CPD App was created to solve a long-standing problem in professional development: fragmented, static and disconnected CPD systems. This case study explains the full journey — from identifying the gap to designing, building and launching a modern CPD platform.

The Gap

  • APM & BCS only provide competency frameworks and static CPD logs.
  • No evidence linking CPD to competency ratings.
  • No development planning or gap analysis.
  • No linkage between CPD log and competency framework.

The Market

  • Personal individuals
  • CPD providers (APM, BCS)
  • Education sector
  • Employers

Current Features

  • Lifecycle framework
  • CPD log with AI suggestions
  • Competency ratings
  • Dashboard & KPIs
  • Awards & badges
  • Gap analysis
  • Evidence vault
  • Development plan
  • Career simulator
  • To‑do list
  • Full reporting suite
  • Profile page
  • Link to CV App

Development Roadmap

  • Corporate & personal packages
  • LinkedIn integration
  • Badge level on profile
  • Social sharing
  • Light/dark mode
  • Calendar scanning (Gmail/Outlook)
