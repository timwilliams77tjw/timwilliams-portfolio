Cloud Security Infrastructure Public Sector
Overview We partnered with the Home Office to deliver a complete redesign, build and implementation of a new outsourced IT infrastructure platform. The programme modernised the Home Office's technology estate by introducing a secure public‑sector cloud environment, a fully managed device ecosystem, and integrated access to critical business applications across a nationwide secure WAN. The Challenge The Home Office required a modern, secure, scalable IT platform capable of supporting diverse operational needs across multiple sites. The legacy environment suffered from fragmented infrastructure and inconsistent security controls, ageing devices with limited remote‑working capability, siloed application access across partner organisations, increasing pressure to meet stringent public‑sector security standards, and a need for unified management across desktops, laptops, tablets and handheld devices. The organisation needed a complete technology refresh, delivered within a secure cloud service and capable of supporting thousands of users across a protected national WAN. Programme Mandate We were commissioned to design, build and implement a new outsourced IT infrastructure platform; deliver the solution within a secure public‑sector cloud environment; provide end‑to‑end secure services across all sites in scope; integrate access to critical business applications supplied by multiple delivery partners; and ensure full compliance with government security and operational standards. This required a multi‑partner delivery model, strong governance, and a robust technical architecture. Platform Design & Architecture We engineered a secure cloud‑based platform that became the foundation for all services delivered across the estate. Key architectural components included secure public‑sector cloud hosting (resilient, compliant, and scalable), a unified device ecosystem spanning desktops, laptops, tablets and handheld devices, secure WAN connectivity with encrypted links across all operational sites, centralised identity and access management for consistent authentication and policy enforcement, and an interoperability framework enabling seamless access to partner‑provided applications. The architecture ensured security, performance, and future scalability. Secure Digital Services Delivered Across the secured WAN, we deployed a suite of fully managed services:
  • Secure Email Services — protected communication across departments and partners.
  • Remote Access — enabling flexible, policy‑driven working.
  • Mobile Device Management (MDM) — full lifecycle control of mobile endpoints.
  • Managed & Hosted Device Services — provisioning, patching, compliance and support.
  • Application Access Integration — secure connectivity to critical business systems delivered by partners.
These services were unified under a single governance and operational model. Multi‑Partner Delivery Model The programme required coordinated delivery across multiple organisations. We led architectural governance, security assurance, infrastructure build and deployment, device rollout and management, integration with partner‑provided applications, and testing, transition and service onboarding. This collaborative approach ensured consistent standards and a seamless user experience. Key Outcomes The programme delivered a modern, secure cloud platform replacing legacy infrastructure, a unified device estate supporting desktops through to handheld devices, and a consistent security posture across all sites and user groups. Operational resilience was improved through cloud‑based hosting, mobility was enhanced via secure remote access and MDM, and integrated access to critical business applications from multiple partners was achieved.
