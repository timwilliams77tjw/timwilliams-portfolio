Overview

Why this wheel exists

The PMO Operating Model Wheel provides a complete, visual representation of how a modern PMO delivers value. It brings together governance, planning, people, and strategic outcomes into a single, coherent framework that leaders can understand at a glance.

Use it to communicate PMO purpose, define scope, support maturity assessments, and anchor conversations with executives, delivery teams, and stakeholders.

At the centre of the wheel sits the PMO’s mission: Enabling Predictable, Value‑Driven Delivery.

Each domain radiates from this purpose, ensuring the PMO operates as a strategic partner rather than a reporting function. The wheel can be used in:

  • Executive briefings and board packs
  • PMO service catalogues and charters
  • Capability and maturity assessments
  • Onboarding and training materials
